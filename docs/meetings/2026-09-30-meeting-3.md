## Proposed Ideas: 

 **Timu**
**Endo Health Personal Assistant App**
A personal health assistant designed specifically for individuals managing endometriosis. Instead of just tracking periods, the app helps users log pain flare-ups, daily diet, movement, cycle phases, and medication schedules to identify personal health triggers and manage daily wellness. 
Daily Symptom & Trigger Hub: Simple logging for pain severity (1–10), daily meals/foods, physical activity/movement, and cycle status.
Medication & Supplement Reminder: Simple checklist/reminder system for daily pills or treatments.
Trigger Analytics Dashboard: Visual graphs matching flare-up spikes against food, movement, and cycle days.
AI Trigger Insights: LLM-assisted summary analyzing recent entries to spot potential symptom triggers (with offline fallback).

**Internship applications tracker**
Target audience: Undergraduate students (US) looking for summer internships


Problem:

Possibly if we can/want to make it more difficult, we can add mb add web scraping (so that when user pastes the link, we fill out these fields automatically for them) idk how doable that is though I’ve never done it
Also maybe we could add a CV manager where you could have all the versions of your CV ready to download with short descriptions of which of your skills do they emphasize the most,  so that when you apply, you don’t have to browse through all your versions of CV again


**Financial planner**

Target audience: Young adults who want to take care of their finances; people who have just started saving up or investing and want to track their goals
Problem: Most of the trackers I see on the internet work retroactively (you set your bands on how much do you want to spend on which category and then you log your spendings into categories and get a monthly summary of how much you spent in each category) but there is no scenario-planning
I would like to for example know if it is better for me to now invest 20% of my salary in stocks or pay off my student loans - so have a sort of dashboard which would show where you are right now given your specific conditions (student loan/no student loan/do you have to support yourself of are your parents supporting you etc) and have different scenarios: how much in savings will you have if you save X amount of money per month, how much will you have if you invest X into this etf etc
Product:
First, a questionnaire of your goals and current financial situation (expected payouts, goals, loans, recurring expenses etc)
Then, a personalized dashboard with 3 different scenarios
And a button to log progress (like paying towards a loan etc)

## 💡 Decisions Made
* **Selected Topic:** **Internship Applications Tracker** tailored for undergraduate students in the US.
* **Core Value Proposition:** Prioritizing job applications based on rolling deadlines and upcoming closing dates.
* **Key Features Agreed Upon:**
  1. **Home Dashboard:** Stats overview and "Closing Today" urgent application banners.
  2. **Application Tracker:** Board view with priority-based deadline sorting.
  3. **CV Version Manager:** Uploading, linking CVs to applications, and custom PDF renaming (`CV_Name_Role.pdf`).
  4. **Social Network:** Friend request system, dashboard sharing, and privacy toggles.
  5. **AI Assistant:** Cover letter / resume feedback tips (with offline fallback).
